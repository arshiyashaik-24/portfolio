About

I am a second-year double-degree student in Computer Science & Engineering and Applied Physics at TU/e. My work spans machine learning, simulation, robotics and software engineering, and it usually follows one habit: build the model, test it against a known answer, and say how far it can be trusted.

This summer I worked as an Imaging and Machine Learning Intern at Canon Production Printing, building interpretable models that detect and classify nozzle anomalies on printhead nozzle plates. In my own projects I have simulated fiber Bragg grating sensors, supersonic jets and the schlieren images they produce, and market making under inventory risk, and built a full-stack search app with an explainable ranking.

I am looking for internships for summer 2027 in engineering, quantitative or software roles where physics and computing meet.
